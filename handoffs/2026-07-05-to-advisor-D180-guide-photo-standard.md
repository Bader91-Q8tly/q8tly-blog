## Draft checklist (summary — full text in the standard file)
- **Safe zone:** the hero is re-cropped across 16:9 → 16:10 → 3:2 → ~1:1; the subject must
  sit centred, ≥~15% margin all sides, never touching an edge (esp. top).
- **Hero:** name-bearing storefront legible in 16:9 AND 3:2 AND ~1:1; OR an intentional,
  clean, landscape food/setting hero.
- **Set:** ≥1 hero + ≥3 body photos that survive 7:5 / ~1:1; **consistent ratios**; subjects
  off the edges.
- **Body:** whole dish in-frame; no competing brand dominating; workable exposure.
- **Outcome:** portrait sources don't auto-fail — **pre-crop rescue** (hero 16:9 / inline
  7:5, centred) is valid if the subject is in the safe zone (note the crop in the ledger).
  Fail with no rescue → **DEFER**; already-published defer = owner's per-case call.
