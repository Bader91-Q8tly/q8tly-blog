guide frame cleanly; else the guide is **DEFERRED** (listing stays live) and we move on.

## Application 1 — B+F (the composition test case)
**The owner's test — "can a re-crop/re-shoot make the sign sit well?" — answer: YES.**
The pre-crop already achieved it: hero **2914** places the "B+F" sign legible, prominent and
well-composed (greenery left, sign on the arch, warm interior right). **Composition = PASS**;
a "needs a better photo" verdict is **not** warranted on composition grounds — the sign sits
well.
**Two honest gaps against the landed numbers (not composition):**
- **Resolution:** every B+F source is **1086 px** wide → under the **1200 px** bar.
- **One ratio:** hero is **16:9 (1.78)**, body photos are **1.40** → two ratios, and 1.78 is
  above the 1.4–1.6 band.
**Recommendation:** keep B+F **live** (it renders cleanly at 1086 px). For full D-180
compliance it wants (a) the hero re-aligned to the guide's **1.40**, and (b) ideally a
**≥1200 px re-shoot at one landscape ratio** on a future pass. Neither is urgent; retire/hold
is the owner's call, but nothing here forces a defer — the *photograph* is good.

## Cross-cutting finding (matters for the queue)
The **≥1200 px + landscape** bar is the real gate: **portrait phone sources ~1080–1150 px
wide cannot yield a compliant landscape crop** (the crop is only as wide as the source). B+F
(1086), south-avenue (1152) and MizuMesa's inline-2 (900) all bump this. Incoming guides
should be shot/collected **landscape at ≥1520 px** or they'll defer on resolution regardless
of composition. Worth stating to whoever sources guide photos (Pipeline/photography lane).
