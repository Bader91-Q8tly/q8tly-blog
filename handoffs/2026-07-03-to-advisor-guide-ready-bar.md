# Briefing → Advisor — the "guide-ready" bar for business-sourced places (2026-07-03)

**From:** Claude Blog (Module 8 — Editorial / Guide). **To:** Advisor → relay to whoever
runs the mall-business outreach (intake / Pipeline-facing).
**Context:** Owner is opening direct outreach to major mall businesses. This is the
editorial companion spec — what a business's cooperation can and can't do toward its
*guide* (as opposed to its *listing*), kept D-168/D-145-clean so intake never blurs the line.

---

## The bar, in one line
**A listing is built from data. A guide is built from our own visit.** Outreach can make
a business easier to *reach* and easier to *verify* — it can never be the *source* of guide
prose. No guide ships from marketing material alone, no matter how cooperative the business
is or how fast the owner wants it live. The rail is D-145 (guides are human-authored, never
lifted from a business's own copy) run through the standing D-168 flow: **Content Writer
drafts from the blogger formula, from our own visit → Bader full-reads EN+AR → Blog injects.**
Outreach doesn't create a shortcut around that gate; it only feeds what the visit needs to go
find.

## What business outreach CAN contribute
Treat everything below as a **lead to verify on our visit**, not a quote to lift:
- **The story of the place** — why it opened, what they'd point to as distinctive. Useful for
  the Content Writer to know what to *look for*; it only becomes guide prose if our visit
  independently confirms it's actually true and actually distinctive.
- **Menu/service anchors** — their stated signature dish or flagship service. Same rule: we
  taste/see it ourselves; it's a tip on where to look, not a fact we take on their word.
- **Logistics** (hours, price tier, address, contact, amenities) — fine to take directly as
  **listing** input (Pipeline lane), and useful as a starting point for the guide's key-facts
  table, but still spot-checked against the visit — hours drift, phones change (same discipline
  already applied to Naranj/Anosha's NEEDS-BADER flags).
- **Usable photos**, if offered — can supplement our own, but captions stay ours, neutral, and
  a supplied photo never substitutes for the visit itself.

## What business outreach CANNOT contribute
- **Their own About / marketing copy is never guide prose.** It's an input to the *listing's*
  About paragraph only (Pipeline lane) — it does not cross into the guide body, ever, even
  paraphrased.
- **Superlatives and persuasive claims** ("best in Kuwait," "must-try," "you won't regret it")
  are banned from guide voice per D-168 regardless of source — a business saying it doesn't
  make it citable, and Bader's sign-off doesn't waive the platform's neutral-voice rule.
- **Outreach cannot pre-empt the Bader full-read gate.** Faster cooperation from the business
  does not mean a faster guide — the visit, the Content Writer draft, and the full EN+AR
  read happen regardless of how the lead came in.
- **Unverifiable business claims don't enter the guide at all** — they can sit in the listing
  flagged unconfirmed (the existing NEEDS-BADER pattern) but never become guide prose on the
  business's word alone.

## Routing, so intake never blurs the line
Outreach conversation notes are **listing intake, Pipeline's input** — they queue the
business for a `gd_place`, not for a guide. A guide only enters the pipeline once an actual
visit is scheduled; the resulting Content Writer MD should carry a visit basis (date/visitor),
same provenance discipline already used in existing guide intakes. **One live implication of
the mall push specifically:** a "guide to [Mall]" doesn't lower this bar for the venues it
features — each venue inside a multi-venue guide clears the same visit-gate individually: no
venue rides into a guide on outreach material just because it shares a `located_in` container
with one that was actually visited.
