## What is matcha?

Matcha is Japanese green tea in powdered form. The tea plants are shaded from the sun for the last few weeks before harvest, then the leaves are ground into a fine, bright green powder. With most tea you steep the leaves and throw them away. With matcha the powder is whisked straight into water or milk, so you drink the whole leaf. That's why it tastes stronger than ordinary green tea: grassy, slightly bitter, and smooth when the quality is good.

## Matcha latte, iced or ceremonial: how it's served

- **Matcha latte.** The one most people order in Kuwait. The powder is whisked with a little water, then topped with hot or cold milk; most cafés offer a plant milk too.
- **Iced matcha.** The same drink over ice, and the summer default. Some places layer it, milk at the bottom and matcha on top.
- **Ceremonial matcha.** The traditional Japanese way: powder and hot water only, whisked in a bowl with a bamboo whisk until it foams. Nothing hides the tea, so this is where the quality of the powder shows most.
- **Flavours.** Kuwait's cafés experiment. Expect strawberry, maple, ube, lavender, rose-cardamom, salted vanilla, coconut, and matcha boba.

## Buying matcha powder in Kuwait

If you want to make matcha at home, check these before you buy:

- **Grade.** Ceremonial grade is for drinking with water alone; it's smoother and costs more. Latte or culinary grade is for mixing with milk and baking, and it's cheaper.
- **Colour.** Good matcha is bright, vivid green. Yellowish or brownish powder is usually old or lower grade.
- **Origin.** Most well-known matcha is Japanese, from regions such as Uji, Nishio and Kagoshima, and most tins name the region.
- **Date and size.** Buy a small tin and check the packing date. Matcha loses colour and flavour quickly once it's opened.
- **Storage.** Keep it sealed in the fridge, away from light and strong smells, and use it within a few weeks.
- **Tools.** A bamboo whisk and a bowl make the foam easier, but a small handheld milk frother is a fine start.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is matcha?

Matcha is Japanese green tea ground into a fine powder and whisked into water or milk. You drink the whole leaf rather than an infusion of it.

### Does matcha have caffeine?

Yes, matcha contains caffeine. How much depends on how much powder goes in and on its grade, and many people find it feels gentler than coffee.

### What are the benefits of matcha?

Because you drink the whole tea leaf, matcha carries the antioxidants found in green tea, along with L-theanine, an amino acid found in tea. It's a drink, not a treatment. If you have a health condition or are sensitive to caffeine, check with your doctor.
