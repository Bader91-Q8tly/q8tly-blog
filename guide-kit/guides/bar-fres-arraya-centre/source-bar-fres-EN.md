# Bar Frès: How Kuwait's Parisian-Japanese Conveyor-Belt Restaurant Works

Bar Frès is one of the newer restaurants in Kuwait City, and it works differently from almost anywhere else in town. Instead of ordering from a menu, you sit at a long marble table while small plates of Japanese food glide past you on a moving belt. See something you like, take it. At the end, you pay for the plates you picked.

Here's everything you need to know before you go: how it works, what the plate colours mean, what a meal costs and who it suits.

## Where it is

Bar Frès is on the ground floor of **Arraya Centre** in Kuwait City. The entrance is easy to spot from the mall: dark wood, red velvet curtains and "bar frès, parisian·japanese" in lights above the door.

## How it works

The heart of the restaurant is the belt. It runs down the middle of long marble tables, with red velvet chairs lined up on both sides.

1. **Sit down** at any free seat along the belt.
2. **Watch the plates go by.** Sushi rolls, salads, noodle bowls and more pass in front of you, each on a coloured plate.
3. **Take what you want.** Lift a plate off the belt whenever something catches your eye.
4. **Keep your empty plates.** The colour of each plate tells the restaurant what to charge.
5. **Pay at the end** for the plates you took, plus any drinks.

It's a fun way to eat, especially when you're hungry and want to try a bit of everything.

## Plate colours and prices

Each plate colour has its own price, so the colour tells you the cost before you pick it up. On our visit in **September 2026**, the bill showed:

| Plate | Price |
|---|---|
| Red plate | 2.850 KD |
| Red and black bowl | 2.950 KD |
| Black plate | 3.450 KD |
| Magenta plate | 5.450 KD |
| Soft drink | 0.800 KD |

Two people spent **22.100 KD** in total with soft drinks, so plan on roughly **11 to 12 KD per person**. You'll also see blue and green plates on the belt; ask the staff for their prices when you arrive, since prices can change.

**A tip:** magenta plates cost almost twice as much as red ones. If you're watching the bill, glance at the colour before you reach.

## The room

For a conveyor-belt restaurant, Bar Frès is surprisingly elegant. Think crimson velvet chairs, white marble counters, crystal chandeliers wrapped in red fringe, candles along the belt and hand-painted crane murals on the walls. The "Parisian" half of the name shows in the room as much as on the plate.

## Good to know

- **Seating is close together.** Chairs sit side by side along the belt, so you may end up next to people you don't know. It's part of the experience, but not the place for a private conversation.
- **Not suitable for kids.** The layout suits adults better than families with young children.
- **No smoking.**
- **Indoor seating only.**
- **Payment:** KNET, Visa/Mastercard and cash.
- **Hours:** open daily from 11 AM. Check the Q8tly listing for today's closing time.

## Who it's for

Bar Frès works best for **a group of friends** who want to share and try lots of dishes, or **a casual date** with a difference. If you've never tried belt dining, it's worth going at least once for the experience alone.
