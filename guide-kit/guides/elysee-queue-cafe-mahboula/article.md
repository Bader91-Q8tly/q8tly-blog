On the 24th floor of the Park Inn by Radisson in Mahboula, two women-only spots share one rooftop level: **Elysee Beauty Lounge**, a beauty salon, and **Queue Cafe**, a café with sea views over the coast. Because they sit on the same floor, a treatment and a coffee can be one outing rather than two — book a salon appointment, then move to the café for coffee and light bites, with the same floor-to-ceiling views the whole time.

## The setting

It's a rooftop floor, 24 stories up: floor-to-ceiling windows, a geometric screen ceiling, and the sea running along the coast below. Both venues are women-only, and there's valet parking at the hotel.

## Elysee Beauty Lounge — the salon

Elysee Beauty Lounge is the salon side: a women-only beauty lounge sharing the floor with the café, so an appointment and a coffee afterward happen in one visit.

## Queue Cafe — the coffee

Queue Cafe is the café side — coffee and light bites at around 10 KD per person, with indoor and outdoor seating and the same sea views. It's women-only, works for groups, and there's room to sit with a laptop. Hours are 10:00 AM – 8:00 PM.
