## EN — guide

**About** *(this paragraph is also the listing About — LOCKED by Bader):*

Queue Café is a women-only rooftop café on the 24th floor of the Park Inn by Radisson in Mahboula, with sea views over the coast. It shares the floor with Elysee Beauty Lounge, so a beauty appointment and a coffee work as one visit. The menu is coffee and light bites, at around 10 KD per person, with indoor and outdoor seating and valet parking.
