Looking for the best burger in Kuwait? These are 14 burger spots on Q8tly, from smash burgers in Shuwaikh to Wagyu sliders in a Kuwait City tower and a drive-thru that stays open until 3 AM. Our top 5 comes first, then the full list by style and by area, with the hours for each.

## Our top 5 burger spots in Kuwait

- **5. [Supernova](/places/shuwaikh/supernova/), Shuwaikh:** a short menu built on two smashed beef patties.
- **4. [Signature](/places/sabah-al-salem/signature-sabah-al-salem/), Sabah Al Salem:** American burgers, open 24 hours.
- **3. [Thinnies](/places/hawalli/thinnies/), Hawalli:** smash burgers in Al Andalus Mall.
- **2. [Jimmy's Smashburgers & Fries](/places/shuwaikh/jimmys-smashburgers-fries-shuwaikh/), Shuwaikh:** smash burgers made with local beef.
- **1. [Milk Bun](/places/shuwaikh/milk-bun-shuwaikh/), Shuwaikh:** American burgers in EDGE Complex, open until 2 AM.

The full list follows, grouped by style. Every place on it lists burgers among its cuisines on Q8tly, and the hours come from each listing.

## Smash burgers

### Supernova, Shuwaikh

[[image:inline-1||Supernova's dining room in Shuwaikh: wooden booths with cream seats along a long white counter, under a curved ceiling.]]

[Supernova](/places/shuwaikh/supernova/) in Al Tilal Complex keeps its menu short: a classic and a house cheeseburger, each with two smashed beef patties, plus fries, cheese sauce and sundaes. The beef is halal and comes from the UAE. You order at the counter and eat in a dining room with booths, daily from noon until 2 AM.

### Jimmy's Smashburgers & Fries, Shuwaikh

[[image:inline-2||The green shopfront of Jimmy's Smashburgers & Fries in Shuwaikh, with "Home of the Smash" written above the glass door.]]

[Jimmy's Smashburgers & Fries](/places/shuwaikh/jimmys-smashburgers-fries-shuwaikh/) is built around smash burgers made with local beef, plus fries. Inside, the room has an American sports theme with basketball memorabilia, and there's outdoor seating in winter. It opens daily at noon and closes at midnight, or 2 AM on Thursdays and Fridays.

### Thinnies, Hawalli

[[image:inline-3||Two Thinnies burgers on a red-lined metal tray: a cheeseburger and a fried chicken burger in a blue wrapper.]]

[Thinnies](/places/hawalli/thinnies/) in Al Andalus Mall serves smash burgers and chicken fillets, with a kids' menu. You can order ahead in the app and collect. Seating is outdoors only, and it's open daily from 9 AM to 11:30 PM.

## American burger restaurants

### Milk Bun, Shuwaikh

[[image:inline-4||The Milk Bun dining room in Shuwaikh, lit red by the neon Milk Bun sign, with round tables and low stools.]]

[Milk Bun](/places/shuwaikh/milk-bun-shuwaikh/) is an American burger restaurant in EDGE Complex, with a spacious, bright room and indoor and outdoor seating. It suits families and groups, and it's open daily from noon until 2 AM. Delivery is available.

### Signature, Sabah Al Salem

[[image:inline-5||Signature's covered outdoor terrace in Sabah Al Salem, with grey cushioned sofas, stone-topped tables and black umbrellas.]]

[Signature](/places/sabah-al-salem/signature-sabah-al-salem/) is an American burger restaurant in Sabah Al Salem that's open 24 hours. There's indoor seating and a covered outdoor terrace.

### SALT, Shuwaikh

[[image:inline-6||SALT's dining room in Shuwaikh: black-and-white striped benches, planters and framed posters on the far wall.]]

[SALT](/places/shuwaikh/salt/) is an American-style restaurant on a busy Shuwaikh street, open daily from 9 AM to midnight for breakfast, lunch and dinner. It's known for its range of fries. There's indoor and outdoor seating with a family section, and it costs around 7 KD per person.

### Chops, Kuwait City

[Chops](/places/kuwait-city/chops/) is an American restaurant on Ahmad Al Jaber Street, open daily from 12:30 PM to 11:30 PM. Service is quick, and there's indoor and outdoor seating with a family section. It doesn't take bookings, it's popular during Eid, and it costs around 8 to 10 KD per person.

## Diners and sliders

### Johnny Rockets, Salmiya

[[image:inline-7||A Johnny Rockets burger with lettuce and a basket of fries on branded paper, on a speckled diner table in Salmiya.]]

[Johnny Rockets](/places/salmiya/johnny-rockets/) is a 1950s-style American diner in Ramez Mall, open 24 hours. The menu includes the Original Burger and the Smoke House, plus crispy chicken tenders, and there's a kids' menu and vegetarian options.

### Slider Station, Kuwait City

[Slider Station](/places/kuwait-city/slider-station/) is a long-standing sliders and small-plates restaurant at the end of the Gulf Road. Its menu is wide, and it's known for its lollipop steaks and Commando Fries. The room is loud and busy, which suits a night out with friends, and it's open from noon until 1 AM, or 2 AM on Thursdays and Fridays.

## Drive-thru and grab-and-go burgers

### Elevation Burger, Masayel

[[image:inline-9||The Elevation Burger branch in Masayel at night, its sign lit above the glass front, with a potted olive tree by the door.]]

[Elevation Burger](/places/masayel/elevation-burger-masayel/) is an American burger franchise, and this branch is next to Masayel Supermarket. It has a drive-thru as well as indoor and outdoor seating, and fresh juice is on the menu. It's open daily from 11 AM to 3 AM.

### WK, Sabah Al Salem

[WK](/places/sabah-al-salem/wk-sabah-al-salem/) is part of a chain, with a wide range of burgers, nachos and sides, and a drink called DAT-WK-GAH. This branch has outdoor seating and a pickup area, but no indoor seating. It's open daily from 8 AM to 2:30 AM.

## Gourmet and steakhouse burgers

### BF BRD, Kuwait City

[[image:inline-10||Outdoor tables along the covered walkway at BF BRD in Khaleejia Tower, Kuwait City.]]

[BF BRD](/places/kuwait-city/bf-brd/) (Beef, Bird & More) is in Khaleejia Tower. Its sliders and burgers are made with Australian Wagyu beef or with chicken, alongside small and big plates. The room is spacious, with soft lighting and American-style décor, and it's open daily from 1 PM to 11 PM.

### Texas Roadhouse, Mahboula

[[image:inline-11||The front of Texas Roadhouse in the Sidra Complex, Mahboula, with outdoor tables and trees in front of the stone facade.]]

[Texas Roadhouse](/places/mahboula/texas-roadhouse/) is an American steakhouse in the Sidra Complex. Burgers are on the menu alongside its steaks, ribs and chicken, and bread is baked fresh and served at the table. It suits families, with high chairs and a kids' menu, and it's open daily from 11 AM to midnight, or 1 AM on Thursdays and Fridays.

## Burgers on a bigger menu

### Gravy, Jahra

[Gravy](/places/jahra/gravy-jahra/) is an Italian restaurant in Jahra that serves burgers alongside its pizza. There's indoor and outdoor seating, and it's open daily from 1 PM to 3 AM.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best burger in Kuwait?

Our pick for the best burger in Kuwait is Milk Bun in EDGE Complex, Shuwaikh, number 1 in our top 5. Numbers 2 to 5 are Jimmy's Smashburgers & Fries in Shuwaikh, Thinnies in Hawalli, Signature in Sabah Al Salem and Supernova in Shuwaikh.

### Where can I find smash burgers in Kuwait?

Supernova in Al Tilal Complex, Shuwaikh, puts two smashed beef patties in each burger. Jimmy's Smashburgers & Fries in Shuwaikh makes its smash burgers with local beef, and Thinnies in Al Andalus Mall, Hawalli, serves smash burgers too.

### Which burger places in Kuwait are open late or 24 hours?

Signature in Sabah Al Salem and Johnny Rockets in Salmiya are open 24 hours. Elevation Burger in Masayel and Gravy in Jahra are open until 3 AM, WK in Sabah Al Salem until 2:30 AM, and Supernova and Milk Bun until 2 AM.

### Is there a burger drive-through in Kuwait?

Yes. Elevation Burger in Masayel has a drive-thru as well as indoor and outdoor seating, and it's open until 3 AM.
