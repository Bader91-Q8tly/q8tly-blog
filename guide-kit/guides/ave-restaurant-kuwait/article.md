AVE is a fine-dining restaurant on 5G Salhiya Street in Kuwait City. It's elegant and upscale, its theme takes you back to the 1980s and early 1990s, and on some nights dinner comes with a live show. It's worth trying for the experience alone: a change of routine and of scene, and a night you won't forget.

## AVE restaurant, Kuwait: fine dining on Salhiya Street

[[image:inline-1||A velvet booth and a marble table at AVE under a tree strung with fairy lights, with patterned wallpaper behind.]]

AVE is upscale and elegant. The decor is very refined, and every corner is prettier than the last. It's listed under [Fine Dining](/places/category/fine-dining/) on Q8tly.

[[image:inline-2||A red-and-white mushroom-shaped dessert with raspberries on a green plate at AVE, with chocolate sauce being poured over it.]]

The food is served with real elegance, and it's delicious.

## Dinner with a show: AVE's theme nights

[[image:inline-3||A neon boombox sign in a corner of AVE, beside a candle-lit table on patterned floor tiles.]]

AVE's theme takes you back to the 1980s and early 1990s, and the staff dress for whichever theme is running. Every so often there are live events and performances, so dinner comes with a show in front of you. The current theme, as of October 2026, is Michael Jackson: his songs play, a screen shows his music videos, and the staff are dressed in Michael Jackson outfits.

## A romantic restaurant in Kuwait, and a night out with friends

[[image:inline-4||A marble table set for two at AVE, with a green-shaded lamp, white armchairs and a wall of greenery and fairy lights behind.]]

The atmosphere at AVE is romantic on some nights and loud on others, and it works for both: it's ideal for married couples and for a group of friends. AVE carries the Date Night tag on Q8tly; the [Date Night page](/places/tags/date-night/) lists the other places with it.

## Birthday and anniversary packages

[[image:inline-5||A table at AVE covered in red rose petals laid out in a heart, with a gold candle holder in the middle.]]

AVE has packages for birthdays and other celebrations, such as a wedding anniversary, and they're something special. The decoration and the cake are elegant and beautifully done. Packages are booked online: the Book Now button on [AVE's Q8tly listing](/places/kuwait-city/ave/) takes you to their booking page.

## Ramadan ghabga at AVE

In Ramadan, AVE hosts great ghabgas, the late-evening gatherings of the holy month, with a full Ramadan theme.

## Late-night dinner: where AVE is, hours and booking

[[image:inline-6||AVE's tiled name sign with a red heart, hung on a lattice of greenery and fairy lights.]]

AVE stays open late, so it suits anyone who likes a night out. It's on 5G Salhiya Street in Kuwait City. Its hours aren't listed on Q8tly yet, so call ahead before you go: the phone number and the Book Now button are on [AVE's listing](/places/kuwait-city/ave/).
