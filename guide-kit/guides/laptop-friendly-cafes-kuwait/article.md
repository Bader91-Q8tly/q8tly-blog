## Hoffee, Kuwait City

- **Area:** Hamad Tower, Kuwait City
- **Wi-Fi:** yes
- **Vibe:** large and calm
- **Seating:** indoor and outdoor; smoking only outside
- **Hours:** daily, 7 AM to 11 PM

[Hoffee](/places/kuwait-city/hoffee/) is used for working, meetings, studying and reading, and it opens early for people on their way to work. The menu has V60 and other specialty coffees, plus sweets. It's also in our [specialty-coffee guide](/guide/specialty-coffee-kuwait/).

## Secret Brunch, Kuwait City

- **Area:** Salhiya Street, Kuwait City
- **Wi-Fi:** not listed
- **Vibe:** traditional décor and artwork
- **Seating:** traditional Arabic-style seating
- **Hours:** Sunday to Thursday 7 AM to 10 PM; Friday and Saturday 10 AM to 10 PM

People come to [Secret Brunch](/places/kuwait-city/secret-brunch/) to study, read and work on a laptop. It serves brunch, breakfast and coffee, with sweets such as cookies and brownies.

## Huna, Sharq

- **Area:** Ahmad Al Jaber Street, Sharq
- **Wi-Fi:** yes
- **Vibe:** a large, brightly coloured space
- **Seating:** indoors all year, outdoor seating in winter, and a big table for meetings
- **Hours:** daily, 7 AM to midnight

[Huna](/places/sharq/huna/) suits studying or getting work done. The menu covers breakfast and snacks, and there's strong AC, a prayer room, and valet and self-parking.

## Forest Botanical Coffee House, Bneid Al Gar

- **Area:** Bneid Al Gar
- **Wi-Fi:** yes
- **Vibe:** quiet, set up for working and studying
- **Seating:** a large space with an indoor garden, outdoor seating and a separate room for studying and meetings; no smoking inside
- **Hours:** open 24 hours, seven days a week

[Forest](/places/bneid-al-gar/forest-botanical-coffee-house/) is the one for a late study night. The menu covers coffee and snacks. It's also in our [specialty-coffee guide](/guide/specialty-coffee-kuwait/).

## His Majesty The Coffee, Bneid Al Gar

- **Area:** on the seafront, Bneid Al Gar
- **Wi-Fi:** yes, for customers
- **Vibe:** quiet in the mornings, good for studying
- **Seating:** indoor and outdoor; the outdoor tables are at their best in winter
- **Hours:** daily, 6 AM to 11 PM

[His Majesty The Coffee](/places/bneid-al-gar/his-majesty-the-coffee-bneid-al-gar/) is an Italian-style coffee shop with fresh baked goods and matcha on the menu, and a prayer room on site.

## KODO Roastery, Shuwaikh

- **Area:** Ghazali Street, Shuwaikh
- **Wi-Fi:** not listed
- **Vibe:** quiet
- **Seating:** indoor, over two floors, suited to laptop work
- **Hours:** daily, 7 AM to 10 PM

[KODO Roastery](/places/shuwaikh/kodo-roastery/) has a wide coffee range, including pour-overs like V60. It's also in our [specialty-coffee guide](/guide/specialty-coffee-kuwait/).

## Espresso Lab, Salmiya

- **Area:** Marina Mall, Salmiya
- **Wi-Fi:** not listed
- **Vibe:** laptop-friendly, good for studying for a while
- **Seating:** two floors; the upper floor has separate indoor smoking and non-smoking areas
- **Hours:** daily, 6 AM to midnight

[Espresso Lab](/places/salmiya/espresso-lab-marina-mall/) serves coffee, bakery items, cookies, sweets and healthy options, with grab-and-go. It's also in our [specialty-coffee guide](/guide/specialty-coffee-kuwait/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Which cafés in Kuwait are good for studying?

Forest Botanical Coffee House in Bneid Al Gar is quiet and has a separate room for studying, Hoffee in Kuwait City is calm and used for working and studying, and His Majesty The Coffee in Bneid Al Gar is quiet in the mornings. All seven study cafés above are listed on Q8tly as places people use for studying or working.

### Which study cafés in Kuwait are open 24 hours?

Of the seven, Forest Botanical Coffee House in Bneid Al Gar is open 24 hours, seven days a week. Huna in Sharq and Espresso Lab in Salmiya stay open until midnight.

### Do these cafés have Wi-Fi?

Hoffee, Huna, Forest Botanical Coffee House and His Majesty The Coffee list Wi-Fi on Q8tly. For Secret Brunch, KODO Roastery and Espresso Lab, Wi-Fi isn't listed, so ask at the counter.
