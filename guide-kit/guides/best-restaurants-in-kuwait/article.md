This guide brings together ten of the best restaurants in Kuwait from across the country, from Nikkei and Syrian to Kuwaiti, Indian and Korean.

They're numbered to make the list easy to read, not ranked against each other. Each one is here because it does something especially well, so pick the one that suits your plans.

## 1. MizuMesa: Nikkei dining in Sharq

MizuMesa sits inside Al-Shaheed Tower in the KIPCO area of Kuwait City and serves Nikkei cuisine: a Japanese cooking base with ingredients and flavours from Peru. It isn't just "Japanese with a twist". The Peruvian influence shows clearly in both the dishes and the plating.

**What to order:** the beef dishes are among the standouts, along with Nikkei plates that combine sushi, sauces and bold flavours.

**Good to know:** upscale, open for lunch and dinner, with indoor seating, a family section and room for groups. Parking at KIPCO Tower is easy. A strong pick for a date night.

## 2. Solange Maison: upscale Chinese in the Salhia Complex

Solange Maison is an upscale Chinese restaurant in the Salhia Complex, designed around a Chinese theme. Seating is indoors and outdoors within the complex, the AC is strong, and some evenings feature live music.

**Best for:** formal occasions, group dinners and date nights. This is one of the most elegant rooms in Kuwait City.

**Good to know:** serves lunch and dinner. **Booking is recommended**, so reserve before you go.

[[place:4025]]

## 3. Naranj: Syrian classics in Salmiya

Naranj brings old Syrian design to Salmiya, with an indoor waterfall fountain at the centre of the room and comfortable, well-spaced seating. The service is attentive, and the waiters are genuinely eager to help.

**What to order:** the meat arayes are a standout.

**Good to know:** the kitchen sends out a complimentary dessert at the end of the meal. A lovely choice for a relaxed family dinner.

[[place:2239]]

## 4. Cure: fusion in the Design District, Shuwaikh

Cure is an upscale restaurant inside the Design District, a lifestyle shopping centre in Shuwaikh. The menu covers Japanese, Mediterranean and fusion dishes, with seasonal specials that change through the year.

**Best for:** date night, especially if you'd like to sit outside.

**Good to know:** indoor and outdoor seating, and reservations are available.

[[place:3807]]

## 5. Off the Coal: the birthday restaurant in Kuwait City

Off the Coal serves a multi-cuisine fusion menu that spans American, Mexican and Japanese dishes, for lunch and dinner. What makes it special is its digital display board, which can be personalised with a guest's name and occasion.

**Best for:** birthdays, anniversaries, weddings and graduations. If you're looking for one of the best restaurants in Kuwait for a birthday, this is it.

**Good to know:** indoor seating with strong AC, and a large open parking area.

[[place:4330]]

## 6. Amiti Noura: traditional Kuwaiti food in Sabah Al-Salem

Amiti Noura serves traditional Kuwaiti dishes in a setting that feels Kuwaiti through and through, from the décor to the music. It's a great place to try local food the way families enjoy it at home.

**Best for:** traditional Kuwaiti food, and breakfast, served from 9 AM to noon.

**Good to know:** family section and dedicated parking, with several coffee shops right next door for after the meal.

[[place:3145]]

## 7. Chef Pillai: Indian with global touches in Abu Halifa

Chef Pillai mixes Indian flavours with global dishes like pastas and other mains. It has an indoor dining room and a family section.

**What to order:** the signature sambar pani puri with pickled mangoes.

**Good to know:** every meal ends with complimentary sulemani tea. One of the best Indian restaurants in Kuwait if you're in the south.

[[place:2818]]

## 8. Kiwa: Korean and Japanese at The Boulevard, Salmiya

Kiwa is a Korean and Japanese restaurant inside Singarea Supermarket at The Boulevard in Salmiya. Because it's inside the supermarket, you can pair your meal with a browse through Singarea's Korean and Japanese groceries and products.

**Best for:** a set lunch, or a private dinner in one of its two private rooms.

**Good to know:** all seating is indoors. Baby chairs are available, though there's no kids menu.

[[place:1939]]

## 9. Queens: all-day fusion at The Avenues

Queens is a fusion restaurant in The Avenues, Al-Rai, with a varied international menu of around 28 dishes, served from breakfast through late dinner.

**Best for:** families and larger groups, at almost any time of day.

**Good to know:** seating inside the restaurant and in the enclosed, air-conditioned mall concourse, with WiFi. Baby chairs, plus valet and self-parking.

[[place:4659]]

## 10. Chops: casual American in Kuwait City

Chops is an American restaurant in Kuwait City, open for lunch and dinner from 12:30 PM to 11:30 PM. Service is quick, and it's a popular spot during Eid and other occasions.

**Best for:** a casual meal, around 8 to 10 KD per person.

**Good to know:** indoor and outdoor seating with a family section. **No advance bookings**, so it's walk-in only. Delivery is available.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which is the best restaurant in Kuwait?

It depends on the occasion. For an upscale dinner, MizuMesa and Solange Maison are two of the best restaurants in Kuwait. For Syrian food, Naranj in Salmiya. For traditional Kuwaiti food, Amiti Noura in Sabah Al-Salem.

### What is the most famous food in Kuwait?

Machboos, spiced rice with meat, chicken or fish, is widely considered Kuwait's national dish. For traditional Kuwaiti cooking, try a Kuwaiti restaurant such as Amiti Noura.

### Which restaurant in Kuwait is best for a birthday?

Off the Coal in Kuwait City can personalise its digital display board with the guest's name and occasion, which makes it a favourite for birthdays and anniversaries.

### Do I need to book?

It varies. Solange Maison recommends booking, Cure takes reservations, and Chops is walk-in only. Check each restaurant's Q8tly page before you go.
