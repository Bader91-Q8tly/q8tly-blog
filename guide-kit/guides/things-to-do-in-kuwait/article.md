Looking for things to do in Kuwait? These are 13 places to visit in Kuwait, from Kuwait Towers and the Grand Mosque to the old souq, museums, parks and the waterfront, and each one comes with restaurants and cafés on Q8tly that are close by. Hours and prices come from each place's official website or account, so check before you go.

## Landmarks and culture

### Kuwait Towers

[[image:inline-1||Kuwait Towers in silhouette against an orange dusk sky, reflected in the calm sea.]]

*Photo: [Samarstha45](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kuwait_tower_at_dusk.jpg) / Wikimedia Commons / [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)*

Kuwait Towers is the country's best-known landmark, three towers on Arabian Gulf Street that opened in 1979. The viewing sphere sits more than 120 metres above the sea and turns a full circle every half hour, and it has a cafeteria for snacks and drinks. The towers are open from 8 AM to 11 PM and the sphere from 2 PM to 11 PM, according to the operator, the Touristic Enterprises Company, and hours can change on holidays. Go in the late afternoon to see the view in daylight and again after dark. [Check before you go](https://www.tec.com.kw/Attractions/detail/Kuwait-Tower) (the page is in Arabic).

**Eat nearby:** [Amimoto](/places/kuwait-city/amimoto/), Japanese, on the towers' lower floor · [Fish Market](/places/kuwait-city/fish-market/), seafood, on the waterfront next to the towers · [Mais Alghanim](/places/sharq/mais-alghanim/), a long-running Lebanese restaurant on the Sharq waterfront

### Souq Al Mubarakiya

[[image:inline-9||The covered main lane of Souq Al Mubarakiya in daylight, hung with Kuwaiti flags, with shopfronts on both sides.]]

*Photo: [Zairon](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kuwait_City_Souq_al-Mubarakeya_3.jpg) / Wikimedia Commons / [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)*

[Souq Al Mubarakiya](/places/qibla/souq-al-mubarakiya/) is one of Kuwait's oldest markets, in the heart of Kuwait City. Its covered lanes are packed with small shops selling spices, perfumes, oud, dates and sweets, with food stalls, cafés and Kuwaiti restaurants in between, and it's all walkable. Go in the late afternoon or evening, when it's cooler.

**Eat nearby:** [Al Shemam](/places/qibla/al-shemam/), Kuwaiti seafood, rice dishes and grills, in the souq · [Bab Al Yemen](/places/qibla/bab-al-yemen/), Yemeni, in the souq · [Assk Kahve](/places/kuwait-city/assk-kahve/), a Turkish-inspired café in the souq

### The Grand Mosque

[[image:inline-2||The south side of the Grand Mosque in Kuwait City: arched sandstone walls behind a row of palm trees, with the dome above.]]

*Photo: [Zairon](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kuwait_City_Grand_Mosque_Exterior_South_Facade_1.jpg) / Wikimedia Commons / [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)*

The Grand Mosque is Kuwait's largest mosque, and its guided tours are the way to see the inside. Tours run Sunday to Thursday, from 9 AM to 1 PM and from 3:30 PM to 7 PM, and the times shift with prayer times. On Fridays and Saturdays, visits are by appointment only. A tour takes 45 to 90 minutes, in Arabic, English or French, and you request one through the Ministry of Awqaf's [online visit form](https://eservices.awqaf.gov.kw/MinistryServicesGate/frames/VisitRequestFormIndividual/form). Sunday to Thursday is simplest, since weekend visits need an appointment. Check before you go on the mosque's [official Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/thegrandmosque/).

**Eat nearby:** [The Grove](/places/kuwait-city/the-grove-kuwait-city/), international, in Sharq · [TAP BAKERY](/places/kuwait-city/tap-bakery/), a Japanese bakery in Al-Sarraf Tower · [Title](/places/kuwait-city/title-kuwait-city/), Italian, on Ahmad Al Jaber Street

### Al Sadu House

Al Sadu House is a 1936 house on Arabian Gulf Street that has been the home of Al Sadu weaving since 1980. Its museum tells the story of Bedouin weaving, from the desert to the town, with pieces such as a traditional tent (the house of hair) and men's cloaks, and there's a shop. The official site lists the house, museum and shop as open from 8 AM to 1 PM and 4 PM to 8 PM, closed on the first two days of Eid, so go in the morning or after 4 PM. [Check before you go](https://alsadu.org.kw/museum/).

**Eat nearby:** [Piatti](/places/kuwait-city/piatti/), Italian, in Khalid Tower on Arabian Gulf Street · [Slider Station](/places/kuwait-city/slider-station/), sliders and small plates, about 300 m away. Souq Al Mubarakiya's restaurants are a short walk too.

### Al Salam Palace Museum

Al Salam Palace, on Arabian Gulf Street near the Sheikh Jaber Al-Ahmad Cultural Centre, opened as a museum in 2019 and holds three museums. You see it on a guided tour, booked online at least 24 hours ahead: the standard tour lasts two hours, in Arabic or English, and costs KD 8. Morning tours run Sunday to Thursday, 9 AM to 2 PM, and evening tours Saturday, Monday and Wednesday, 5 PM to 8 PM; arrive 15 minutes early. Children under 12 aren't allowed. [Check before you go](https://aspm.com.kw/en/tours/).

**Eat nearby:** [Novikov Brasserie](/places/kuwait-city/novikov-brasserie/), Italian, at the Sheikh Jaber Al-Ahmad Cultural Centre, about 700 m away · [White Robata](/places/kuwait-city/white-robata/), Japanese-American robata, at the same centre

### Tareq Rajab Museum, Jabriya

[[image:inline-3||The Gold Room at the Tareq Rajab Museum: glass cases of gold jewellery around a lit central display.]]

*Photo: [Tnrajab](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:TRM_Gold_Room.jpg) / Wikimedia Commons / [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)*

The Tareq Rajab Museum is a private museum of Islamic art in Jabriya, and its second building nearby, the Museum of Islamic Calligraphy, opened in 2007 and holds four Ka'aba door curtains. One ticket covers both: KD 3 for adults, KD 1.5 for under-18s, and free for children under 6. The museum lists its hours as Saturday to Thursday, 9 AM to 1 PM and 4:30 PM to 8:30 PM, and Friday, 9 AM to 1 PM. [Check before you go](https://trmkt.org/).

**Eat nearby:** [Fish Market](/places/salmiya/fish-market-salmiya/), seafood, in Salmiya, about 2 km away · [EDO](/places/salmiya/edo/), Japanese, in Al Khaled Complex, about 2.5 km away

## Waterfront and outdoors

### Arabian Gulf Street

[[image:inline-4||The view from Kuwait Towers over a curved sandy beach, a pier and Arabian Gulf Street, with the city skyline behind.]]

*Photo: [Zairon](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kuwait_City_Kuwait_Towers_View_to_the_Arabian_Gulf_Street_1.jpg) / Wikimedia Commons / [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)*

Arabian Gulf Street, or Gulf Road, follows the coast, and its waterfront runs from Shaab to Bneid Al Gar. Along it, the Touristic Enterprises Company lists sea clubs and public beaches, parks and play areas, sports facilities, and restaurants and cafés, with Kuwait Towers on the way. Walk a stretch or drive the whole road, best early in the morning or after sunset in the cooler months. [See the waterfront page](https://www.tec.com.kw/Attractions/Detail/the-sea-front) (in Arabic).

**Eat nearby:** [Slider Station](/places/kuwait-city/slider-station/), sliders and small plates, at the end of the Gulf Road in Kuwait City · [Babel](/places/salmiya/babel/), Lebanese, with Gulf views in Salmiya. Slider Station is also in our [best burger in Kuwait](/guide/best-burger-in-kuwait/) guide.

### Al Shaheed Park

[[image:inline-5||A curving paved path through Al Shaheed Park at night, lit by modern streetlights between trees and lawns.]]

*Photo: [Coretto958](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:A_pathway_with_modern_streetlights_and_trees_in_Al_Shaheed_Park,_Kuwait.jpg) / Wikimedia Commons / [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)*

Al Shaheed Park is Kuwait's largest park, with lawns, lakes and paths in the middle of Kuwait City. It has two museums, the Memorial Museum and the Habitat Museum, which covers Kuwait's wild birds and plants. The park is open every day from 5 AM to midnight, and the museums open Saturday to Thursday. Smoking, bikes and pets aren't allowed inside. Mornings and evenings are the best times for a walk. [Check before you go](https://alshaheedpark.kw/en/).

**Eat nearby:** [Fired](/places/kuwait-city/fired/), Italian and pizza, in The H Tower · [Odachi](/places/kuwait-city/odachi/), Japanese, in Khaleejia Tower · [Neighbors Matcha](/places/kuwait-city/neighbors-matcha/), a matcha café opposite Al Asima Mall

## Places to visit in Kuwait with family

These are fun places to go in Kuwait with children, and all three are indoors.

### The Scientific Center, Salmiya

[[image:inline-6||A large shoal of silver fish swimming in a tank at the Scientific Center's aquarium in Salmiya.]]

*Photo: [irvin calicut](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:View_from_kuwait_scientific_centre_in_salmiya_%2820%29.jpg) / Wikimedia Commons / [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/)*

The Scientific Center on the Salmiya seafront has an aquarium covering the desert, the coastal edge and Kuwait's largest marine tank, with sharks and sea turtles, plus an IMAX cinema, The Power of Play and World of Science. Its website gives opening hours of 9 AM to 10 PM, and its FAQ lists Friday opening at 2 PM. Tickets are sold online or at the entrance. [Check before you go](https://tsck.org.kw/).

**Eat nearby:** [Naranj](/places/salmiya/naranj/), Syrian, on Arabian Gulf Street · [Toby's Estate (Salmiyah)](/places/salmiya/tobys-estate-salmiyah/), a specialty coffee roastery in Symphony Mall

### Sheikh Abdullah Al Salem Cultural Centre

The Sheikh Abdullah Al Salem Cultural Centre is a museum complex that opened in 2018, with museums of natural history, science, space and Arabic Islamic science, and a planetarium. Tickets are KD 3 for adults, KD 2 for under-18s and KD 1.5 for over-60s, children under 2 go free, and planetarium shows cost KD 2 more at the museum. The centre lists its hours as Monday to Thursday and Saturday, 9 AM to 10 PM, and Friday, 2 PM to 10 PM, with last entry at 9 PM. You can buy tickets online. [Check before you go](https://booking.ascckw.com/).

**Eat nearby:** [Untold](/places/kuwait-city/untold/), a specialty coffee café in Shaab, about 1 km away · [OKAN](/places/salmiya/okan-salmiya/), a small Kuwaiti coffee café in Salmiya · [EDO](/places/salmiya/edo/), Japanese, in Al Khaled Complex

### The Avenues, Al Rai

[[image:inline-8||The Prestige district of The Avenues: palm trees under a glass dome, shopfronts on two floors and escalators in the foreground.]]

*Photo: [Jassem1992](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Prestige_the_avenues_mall_of_Kuwait.jpg) / Wikimedia Commons / [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)*

[The Avenues](/places/rai/the-avenues/) in Al Rai is a mall of 12 districts with more than 1,100 stores, and being indoors, it works in any weather. [VOX Cinemas](/places/rai/vox-cinemas/) is inside. The mall asks visitors to dress respectfully, and pets aren't allowed. Its website shows the day's opening hours, so [check before you go](https://www.the-avenues.com/kuwait/en).

**Eat nearby:** [Em Sherif Café](/places/rai/em-sherif-cafe/), Lebanese · [Said Dal 1923](/places/rai/said-dal-1923/), a café and sweets · [Wooden Bakery](/places/rai/wooden-bakery-avenues/), a bakery, all three inside the mall

### Indoor activities in Kuwait

For more indoor activities in Kuwait with children, these places are on Q8tly:

- [Below Zero, Marina Mall](/places/salmiya/below-zero-marina-mall/): skating rinks and snow play, with skates provided and supports to help children skate.
- [Jumparoo](/places/hawalli/jumparoo/) and [Wonderkin](/places/hawalli/wonderkin/), Al Andalus Mall, Hawalli: soft play and climbing, and paid sessions such as slime and painting.
- [VOX Cinemas, The Avenues](/places/rai/vox-cinemas/): an IMAX hall and two KIDS halls.
- [Fun City, The Gate Mall](/places/egaila/fun-city-gate-mall/): arcade games, VR and a children's playground.

## Places to visit in Kuwait at night

Kuwait Towers stays open until 11 PM and Al Shaheed Park until midnight, and the Scientific Center and the Sheikh Abdullah Al Salem Cultural Centre are open until 10 PM. Two more places are at their best after dark.

### Marina Crescent, Salmiya

Marina Crescent is the seafront side of [Marina Mall](/places/salmiya/marina-mall/), joined to the mall by a glass bridge and lined with cafés and restaurants, in a complex that also has the Marina Yacht Club. The mall's shops open from 10 AM to 10 PM on weekdays and until 11 PM at weekends, and the restaurants inside from 9 AM to 11 PM. Come in the evening for dinner by the water. [Check before you go](https://www.marinaworld.com.kw/opening-hours).

**Eat nearby:** [Melt](/places/salmiya/melt-salmiya/), Mediterranean, on the water at Marina Crescent · [Espresso Lab, Marina Mall](/places/salmiya/espresso-lab-marina-mall/), specialty coffee on two floors · [Jihan](/places/salmiya/jihan/), Iranian grills, with a garden and fountain

### Sheikh Jaber Al-Ahmad Cultural Centre

Kuwait's opera house and cultural centre on Arabian Gulf Street opened in 2016, and this season's calendar of concerts, theatre and film runs from September to December. Tickets are sold only through the centre's app and website. There's a dress code: formal or smart casual, with no shorts, caps, sportswear or dishdasha without headwear. Check [what's on](https://www.jacc-kw.com/whats-on) before you go.

**Eat nearby:** [Novikov Brasserie](/places/kuwait-city/novikov-brasserie/), Italian, at the cultural centre · [White Robata](/places/kuwait-city/white-robata/), Japanese-American robata, at the cultural centre

## Frequently asked questions

### What are the best places to visit in Kuwait?

Kuwait Towers, Souq Al Mubarakiya, the Grand Mosque and Al Shaheed Park are the classic places to visit in Kuwait. For museums, there's the Sheikh Abdullah Al Salem Cultural Centre, Al Salam Palace Museum, the Tareq Rajab Museum and Al Sadu House. For the sea, walk Arabian Gulf Street or have dinner at Marina Crescent in Salmiya.

### What are the free things to do in Kuwait?

Arabian Gulf Street's public seafront, the Marina Crescent promenade in Salmiya and The Avenues mall have no entry ticket. Two museums let young children in free: under-6s at the Tareq Rajab Museum and under-2s at the Sheikh Abdullah Al Salem Cultural Centre.

### What are the best places to visit in Kuwait with family?

The Scientific Center in Salmiya has an aquarium with sharks and sea turtles, an IMAX cinema, The Power of Play and World of Science. The Sheikh Abdullah Al Salem Cultural Centre has museums of natural history, science and space, with free entry for children under 2. The Avenues in Al Rai has 12 districts and a VOX cinema with KIDS halls. For indoor activities, there are ice rinks and snow play at Below Zero in Marina Mall and soft play at Jumparoo and Wonderkin in Al Andalus Mall.

### What are the places to visit in Kuwait at night?

Kuwait Towers is open until 11 PM, with the viewing sphere open from 2 PM. Al Shaheed Park is open until midnight. Marina Crescent in Salmiya has restaurants by the water, and the Sheikh Jaber Al-Ahmad Cultural Centre runs evening concerts and theatre.
