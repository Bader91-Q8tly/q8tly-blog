## Romantic dinner with a view

### Nōya, Kuwait City

[[image:inline-1||The dining room at Nōya at sunset: leather booths and set tables along a wall of slanted windows, with Kuwait City below and the sun on the horizon.]]

- **Setting:** the 28th floor of Jassim Tower, Al-Soor Street, with a panoramic view over Kuwait City
- **Cuisine:** international; breakfast until 12 PM
- **Hours:** daily, 8:30 AM to 11 PM
- **Booking:** [Book a table](https://q8tly.com/go/book/noya?from=web&lang=en)

[Nōya](/places/kuwait-city/noya/)'s atmosphere is romantic, with music in the evening. Occasion packages include table decoration, and every so often there are live bands or performance shows. Booking ahead is preferred.

### Amimoto, Kuwait City

[[image:inline-2||A table by the window at Amimoto in Kuwait Towers, with a bamboo plant on the sill and the sea, the beach and the city skyline below.]]

- **Setting:** Kuwait Towers, Arabian Gulf Street, with a sea view from the towers
- **Cuisine:** Japanese and sushi, with a Japanese chef running the kitchen
- **Hours:** 1:30 PM to 10 PM; closed on Tuesdays
- **Booking:** [Book a table](https://q8tly.com/go/book/amimoto?from=web&lang=en) (the Kuwait Towers restaurants' booking page; choose Amimoto there)

[Amimoto](/places/kuwait-city/amimoto/) is upscale and non-smoking, and it takes reservations by phone or online. There's parking for visitors to the towers.

## Anniversary and birthday dinners

### Piatti, Kuwait City

[[image:inline-3||A slice of layered cake with a lit candle at Piatti, on a plate with "Happy Birthday" written in chocolate.]]

- **Setting:** Khalid Tower, Arabian Gulf Street, with indoor and outdoor seating
- **Cuisine:** Italian
- **Hours:** daily, 1:30 PM to 10:30 PM
- **Booking:** [Book a table](https://q8tly.com/go/book/piatti?from=web&lang=en)

[Piatti](/places/kuwait-city/piatti/) is calm, and people use it for celebrations such as birthdays and weddings: the restaurant provides a cake, and you choose the wording on it. Now and then there's live music, with a solo musician or a band. There's no valet; self-parking is in the multi-storey car park behind the tower. It's best to call before you go.

### Roka, Rai

[[image:inline-4||A window table at Roka with red cushions on the bench, and palm trees and the main road outside.]]

- **Setting:** the Waldorf Astoria Kuwait hotel at The Avenues, Rai; indoor seating, with windows over the hotel pool on one side and the main road on the other
- **Cuisine:** Japanese, including sushi
- **Hours:** Sunday to Wednesday 6 PM to 11:30 PM; Thursday to Saturday 1 PM to 11:30 PM
- **Booking:** [Book a table](https://q8tly.com/go/book/roka?from=web&lang=en)

[Roka](/places/rai/roka/) is an upscale Japanese restaurant with set menus for occasions, priced by what you choose. Reservations are accepted, and there's valet parking.

### AVE, Kuwait City

[[image:inline-5||A table at AVE covered in red rose petals laid out in a heart, with a gold candle holder in the middle.]]

- **Setting:** 5G Salhiya Street, Kuwait City; indoor seating
- **Cuisine:** multi-cuisine
- **Hours:** not listed on Q8tly yet, so call ahead
- **Booking:** [Book a table](https://q8tly.com/go/book/ave?from=web&lang=en)

[AVE](/places/kuwait-city/ave/)'s dining room is done in soft lighting, greenery and decorative touches, and Q8tly lists it as good for celebrations. For its theme nights and its birthday and anniversary packages, read our [AVE guide](/guide/ave-restaurant-kuwait/).

## More date night restaurants

### Solange Maison, Kuwait City

[[image:inline-6||A round table at Solange Maison set under a small lamp, in front of a blue-and-white tiled mural of mountains, with a fringed canopy above.]]

- **Setting:** Salhia Complex (mezzanine), Kuwait City, with indoor and outdoor seating within the complex
- **Cuisine:** Chinese
- **Hours:** daily, 1 PM to 11 PM
- **Booking:** [Book a table](https://q8tly.com/go/book/solange?from=web&lang=en)

[Solange Maison](/places/kuwait-city/solange/) is an upscale Chinese restaurant with a Chinese-themed design, and it sometimes has live music. It suits formal occasions and serves lunch and dinner. There's more in our [Solange Maison guide](/guide/solange-maison-kuwait-city/).

### MizuMesa, Sharq

[[image:inline-7||A curved green booth at MizuMesa with two white armchairs, set for a meal under a small lamp, with plants and a window behind.]]

- **Setting:** Al-Shaheed Tower in the KIPCO area, Sharq; indoor seating
- **Cuisine:** Nikkei: a Japanese cooking base with ingredients and flavours from Peru
- **Hours:** daily, 1 PM to 11 PM
- **Booking:** [Book a table](https://q8tly.com/go/book/mizumesa?from=web&lang=en)

[MizuMesa](/places/sharq/mizumesa/) is upscale and works for lunch or dinner. Beef dishes are among the menu's standouts, alongside Nikkei plates that blend sushi, sauces and bold flavours. Parking is easy at KIPCO Tower. There's more in our [MizuMesa guide](/guide/mizumesa-sharq/).

### What are the most romantic restaurants in Kuwait?

Our seven picks are Nōya and Amimoto for dinner with a view, Piatti, Roka and AVE for an anniversary or a birthday, and Solange Maison and MizuMesa for date night. All seven romantic restaurants carry the Date Night tag on Q8tly.

### Which romantic restaurants in Kuwait have a view?

Nōya is on the 28th floor of Jassim Tower, with a panoramic view over Kuwait City. Amimoto is in Kuwait Towers, with a sea view.

### Where can I have an anniversary or birthday dinner in Kuwait?

Piatti provides a cake for celebrations, and you choose the wording on it. Roka has set menus for occasions, Nōya has occasion packages with table decoration, and AVE is listed as good for celebrations; its packages are in our [AVE guide](/guide/ave-restaurant-kuwait/).
