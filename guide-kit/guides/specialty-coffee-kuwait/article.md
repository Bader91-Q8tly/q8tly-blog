## What "specialty coffee" means, and what a V60 is

Specialty coffee means beans that are graded, traceable to where they were grown, roasted with care and brewed fresh. A specialty café usually tells you the origin of the beans and offers more than one way to brew them.

V60 is one of those ways, and the one you'll see most on Kuwait's menus. It's a pour-over method named after its dripper: a V-shaped cone set at a 60-degree angle and lined with a paper filter. Hot water is poured by hand over freshly ground coffee and drips straight into the cup. There's no machine and no milk, so what you taste is the bean. V60 coffee comes out lighter and clearer than espresso. On Q8tly, the cafés that brew it carry a V60 tag, and they're marked below.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best coffee shop in Kuwait?

It depends on what you want. For V60 and a quiet table, look at the V60-tagged cafés in Shuwaikh and Kuwait City. For a coffee at 3 AM, the 24-hour places include % Arabica in Salmiya, Forest in Bneid Al Gar and Lark in Ahmadi. For beans, start with the roasteries above.

### What is V60 coffee?

V60 coffee is filter coffee made by hand in a cone-shaped dripper with a paper filter, named for the cone's 60-degree angle. It's black, lighter than espresso, and shows off the bean's flavour.

### Where is a good coffee shop in Kuwait City?

Untold in Al-Shaab Al-Bahry, Hoffee in Hamad Tower, L8A behind Salhiya Complex and Assk Kahve in Souq Al Mubarakiya are all in Kuwait City, and Untold, Hoffee and Assk Kahve brew V60.
