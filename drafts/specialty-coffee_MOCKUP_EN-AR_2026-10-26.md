## Sample paragraphs (the voice)

**EN, from section 1 (the V60 paragraph that catches «v60 coffee»):**

> V60 is a pour-over method named after its dripper: a V-shaped cone set at a 60-degree angle and lined with a paper filter. Hot water is poured by hand over freshly ground coffee and drips straight into the cup. There's no machine and no milk, so what you taste is the bean. On Q8tly, the cafés that brew it carry a V60 tag. In Hawalli, [Mug Coffee & Roastery](https://q8tly.com/places/hawalli/mug-coffee-roastery-al-andalus-mall/) and [Kofe](https://q8tly.com/places/hawalli/kofe/), both in Al Andalus Mall, serve V60 pour-overs.
