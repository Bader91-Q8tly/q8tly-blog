Anosha Beauty Salon is a beauty salon in Sabah Al-Salem, on the 3rd floor of Bldg 521C — a small, quiet space that runs by appointment rather than walk-in, with the music kept low. It has strong A/C and easy parking behind the building. There's no dedicated prayer room, but on request the staff can set up a private, screened space with a prayer mat, a thobe, and the qibla direction marked.
